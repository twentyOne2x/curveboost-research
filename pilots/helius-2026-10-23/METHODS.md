# Methods and limits

Selection is fixed in inputs/pilot-manifest.json: four per reported delay band, stratified by retained flags, lexicographic mint selection; no subsequent outcomes used. CSV times are source proxies until checked against signed creation, migration instruction/event, derived bonding curve/canonical pool and same-transaction pool creation. Actual first-trade own-hour is [first trade, +3600 seconds).

The analyzer verifies raw response SHA-256 and cursor chains, deduplicates transactions by signature with conflict checks, decodes retained Pump/PumpSwap layouts, excludes failed transactions and preserves ambiguous logs. It binds trades to exact mint/derived canonical pool. All traders remain included. Values remain raw SOL/WSOL lamports, with no invented USD conversion.

The creator fee field is gross and overlaps holder_rewards: never add them. Holder routing is subtracted only for a separate event component; it is not evidence of creator-wallet ownership, accrual recipient or receipt. Cashback is separate. Quote transfers reconcile actual same-invocation token movements.

Unresolved: 94,188 unparsed event tails (mostly eight zero bytes; two nonzero), 244 unterminated log stacks, 20 log-stack mismatches, six post-complete buys requiring a separate phase; 308 bound own-hour events have ambiguous invocation status. Keep these qualifications: full schema/log closure and all-venue completeness are unproved. Do not call subtotals complete creator revenue or causal Boost performance.

The capture summary records only canonical curve/pool streams and provider terminality. The 39 competitor members in the 12 pilot races were not fetched; no complete race reconstruction is claimed. Account-dashboard usage, credential setup, screenshots and browser state are excluded.
