CurveBoost: boost the opening rush

@pumpfun saw 46,528 launches on 22 October 2026, about 32 per minute, according to @tehcscreener. Tokens compete for the same idea within seconds.

We're building CurveBoost at @attndotmarkets for @pumpfun launches. The goal is simple: borrow liquidity to reach the highest creator-fee tier instantly, so all initial trading volume earns that rate.

Opening volume matters

Using a non-exhaustive dataset shared by @Circular_fi (22-23 October 2026), we studied 5,534 tokens across 1,219 contested vamp races. The strongest two-minute opening led first-hour volume in 82.6% of races; the first launch won 47.3%. Each race had two or more tokens above $1,000 in first-hour volume, matched by ticker and similar name or identical image. Windows start at each token's first trade.

What the first hour could have paid

Across 854 first-hour histories, we repriced only bonding-curve volume from @pumpfun's 30 bps (0.30%) to PumpSwap's 95 bps (0.95%) creator-fee tier at 420-1,470 SOL market cap. Existing post-graduation fees stay unchanged; all trades count, including launchers'. With creators keeping the full allocation:

Pigeon (@esotericpigeon), 583 launches: $256,914 more in first-hour creator fees (+66.5%)!

Ram (@ramonos), 271 launches: $83,179 more in first-hour creator fees (+96.1%)!

The same trading activity could generate more creator fees. For plsbro launches, a larger fee pot could also give them a stronger reason to claim it.

A sustainable model for CurveBoosters

The vision is a DeFi product where CurveBoosters provide liquidity to deployers and earn compensation for their capital and launch support.

The business model remains open: retaining inventory, boosting only when volume is sufficient, taking a share of creator fees, or combining these approaches. Its economics depend on @pumpfun's current fee policy.

Capital needs to be recycled. Unless fees justify keeping it deployed, that may require selling all booster inventory, possibly quickly. Traders need visibility into how that inventory is held or sold. Deployers should know the token allocation and circulating supply their own contribution buys.

The working thesis is a better deal for deployers, creators, liquidity providers and traders, with more of each trade's fees going to creators and less to Pump's protocol. Transparent inventory and exit rules are essential.

Part of what we're building

We're building a launchpad for our own products, other founders and launches around people or ideas. Creator fees could support recurring income across those launches. CurveBoost is intended for original tokens, vampire launches and the plsbro meta alike.

We plan to release the underlying launchpad product first and share more soon. CurveBoost is still in development. We'd like feedback from deployers and traders on the incentives, transparency and capital-recycling design.
