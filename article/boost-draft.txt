CurveBoost: boost the opening rush

@pumpfun saw 46,528 launches on 22 October 2026, about 32 per minute, according to @tehcscreener. Tokens compete for the same idea within seconds.

We're building CurveBoost at @attndotmarkets to shift that battleground beyond the bonding curve: bring graduation forward so more opening volume generates higher creator fees.

Opening volume matters

Across 1,219 contested races in @Circular_fi's 22-23 October dataset, the strongest two-minute opening led first-hour volume in 82.6% of races. The first launch won 47.3%. Each race had two or more tokens above $1,000 in first-hour volume, matched by ticker and similar name or identical image. Windows start at each token's first trade.

What the first hour could have paid

Across 854 first-hour histories, we repriced only bonding-curve volume from @pumpfun's 30 bps (0.30%) to PumpSwap's 95 bps (0.95%) creator-fee tier at 420-1,470 SOL market cap. Existing post-graduation fees stay unchanged; all trades count, including launchers'. With creators keeping the full allocation:

Pigeon (@esotericpigeon), 583 launches: 3,604.63 SOL ($386,560) becomes 6,000.32 SOL ($643,474).

Ram (@ramonos), 271 launches: 807.51 SOL ($86,597) becomes 1,583.14 SOL ($169,776).

The same trading activity could generate more creator fees. For launches around someone's work, a larger fee pot could also give them a stronger reason to claim it.

A sustainable model for CurveBoosters

The vision is a DeFi product where CurveBoosters provide liquidity to deployers and earn compensation for their capital and launch support.

The business model remains open: retaining inventory, boosting only when volume is sufficient, taking a share of creator fees, or combining these approaches. Its economics depend on @pumpfun's current fee policy.

Capital needs to be recycled. Unless ongoing fees justify keeping it deployed, recovering capital could require selling the entire booster inventory, potentially quickly. Making that process transparent enough for traders to react, while preserving meaningful exposure for the original deployer, is central to the design. The goal is a fairer battleground with clearer rules for that transition.

Part of what we're building

We're building a launchpad for our own products, other founders and launches around people or ideas. Creator fees could support recurring income across those launches. CurveBoost is intended for original tokens, vampire launches and the "please bro" meta alike.

We plan to release the underlying launchpad product first and share more soon. CurveBoost is still in development. We'd like feedback from deployers and traders on the incentives, transparency and capital-recycling design.
