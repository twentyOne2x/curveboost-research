Boost: built for the opening rush

@pumpfun saw 46,528 launches on 22 October 2026, about 32 per minute on average, according to @tehcscreener.

Tokens routinely compete for the same idea within seconds. We're building Boost at @attndotmarkets around one goal: make more of a launch's opening volume happen after graduation, at the highest creator-fee rate.

Across 1,219 contested launch races in @Circular_fi's 22-23 October 2026 dataset, the token with the strongest own two-minute opening also led first-hour volume in 82.6% of races. The first token launched won 47.3%. Each race had at least two tokens with $1,000 in first-hour volume. Races matched ticker and similar name or identical image; each token's windows start at its own first trade.

Boost is designed to bring graduation forward so that opening activity can generate higher creator fees, instead of spending that window on the bonding curve.

What the first hour could have paid

Our analysis covers 854 complete first-hour trading histories and the creator fees they generated. @pumpfun's creator fees are 30 basis points (0.30%) on the bonding curve, versus 95 basis points (0.95%) in PumpSwap's 420 to 1,470 SOL market-cap tier. We repriced only the bonding-curve volume at that higher rate. Existing post-graduation fees stay unchanged. All trades count, including the original launchers' trades.

With the creator keeping the full fee allocation:

Pigeon (@esotericpigeon), 583 launches: 3,604.63 SOL ($386,560) in recorded first-hour creator fees becomes 6,000.32 SOL ($643,474).

Ram (@ramonos), 271 launches: 807.51 SOL ($86,597) in recorded first-hour creator fees becomes 1,583.14 SOL ($169,776).

The same trading activity pays more to its creator because the bonding-curve portion happens after graduation.

Give the creator a reason to claim

When a token launches around someone's work, the fees are an invitation to get involved. Boost aims to make that invitation worth claiming by building a larger fee pot during the window that matters most.

Whether you assign the fees to another creator or keep them yourself, the goal is the same: more creator fees from the opening volume.

Boost is still in development, and part of our ambition to build the best launchpad at @attndotmarkets. We plan to use it for our own launches too, helping more of their opening volume generate the highest creator fees.
